# Community Code of Conduct

Trayloom welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, patient and constructive.
Discuss ideas and code on their merits, accept feedback, and respect other people's privacy and time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, spam, and publishing someone else's
private information without permission are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, discussions, or other
project community spaces. These expectations also apply when representing the project publicly.

Report concerns privately to **Naveen022006** at **kanagunaveen21@gmail.com**. Include relevant links and context;
avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with information shared
only when needed to investigate or respond.

The maintainer may request edits, remove inappropriate content, close or lock a conversation, or restrict participation
temporarily or permanently depending on the conduct and its severity. Requests for reconsideration can be sent to the
same address. These community rules govern participation; they do not add restrictions to the software license.
